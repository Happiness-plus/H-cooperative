Ask us anything
Can you start from only an idea?

Yes. The Kawaii Kohai plan is designed to help shape an early concept into a focused visual novel foundation with writing, art, audio, programming, and producer guidance.

Can you join a project already in development?

Yes. We can evaluate the existing scripts, assets, code, and production gaps, then recommend a complete package or the specific disciplines needed to finish the work.

Do I retain the commercial rights?

Yes. The cooperative establishes ownership expectations in a pre-work contract so the commercial rights to the commissioned game remain with you.

What makes the custom plan different?

King of Kamige expands beyond the fixed plans with a tailored scope, including options such as fluid animation, professional voice acting, and a fully customized multidisciplinary production.
